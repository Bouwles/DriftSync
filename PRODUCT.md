# DriftSync

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Desktop Python/Pygame application, with headless Python research tools.

## Users and purpose

The supplied brief targets university/research demonstrations and portfolio review.
Users perform a sustained-attention shape task and inspect predicted error risk,
recent behaviour, uncertainty, training evidence and saved sessions.

## Capabilities and constraints

Preserve trial timing, task rules, calibration, feature engineering, model behaviour,
checkpoint discovery, synthetic training, graphs, logs and exports. Prediction means
probability of an error in the configured future trial horizon. MC-dropout standard
deviation is uncertainty, not a calibrated confidence score. Rule-based explanations
describe behavioural signals; they do not prove model causality.

## Evidence

Default training data is synthetic. The project is a research demonstration, not a
clinically validated measurement. Existing models do not output calibrated confidence.
No invented statistics or decorative data charts.

## Brand commitments

DriftSync: calm, precise, technically credible. Charcoal and graphite surfaces,
off-white typography, restrained teal accent. Status colours carry semantic meaning.
